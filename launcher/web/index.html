    Your card

    Everyone in an online match needs different IDs. New card IDs give you a new, empty card. Your old card stays on your card server, and typing the old values back in brings it back.

    Customize your card

    Use the tabs above, or BIG EDITOR for a larger window, to change your pilot name, favorite suits, comms, sticker and buttons. Changes are saved to your card on the card server, and show up the next time you insert your card.

    Play once first so your card exists. Before every change the launcher backs up your card to exvs-launcher\backups\card.

    Pilot

    Letters, numbers, spaces and Japanese work. Symbols like ! ? & are changed to the game's full-width versions.

    Gauge design

    Favorite suits

    Up to 12. These show first on the suit select screen. New suits use your default gauge and BGM.

    Comms messages

    Up to 13 characters each. Up, down, left and right are the comms button plus a direction in battle. The losing side always sends "ありがとうございます" at the end.

    Comms options

    Sending automatically skips the four direction messages in battle. Start and end messages still send.

    Controller buttons in game (Normal Type (Custom) only)

    Pick "Normal Type (Custom)" for the buttons below to apply. These are the game's own button settings on your card. Keyboard and controller numbers for the PC are on the CONTROLS tab.

    Network

    Picked automatically. The game and the Live Monitor both use this adapter (it's written to config.ini and lmnfig.ini). VPN and virtual adapters are hidden because they break the game.

    Advanced

    With advanced options on, HOST appears on the PLAY tab so one PC can be the match server for a group.
